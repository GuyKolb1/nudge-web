Get Nudge for Android

Quick notes and reminders. This is a test version, installed directly from this page and not from Google Play.

Download Nudge (APK)

Open this page on your Android phone. The same link always gives the newest version. New versions install over the old one and keep your notes.

How to install

  1. Tap Download Nudge above. If the browser warns that the file may be harmful, choose Download anyway.
  2. Open the downloaded file nudge.apk (from the notification, or from the Downloads folder).
  3. Android asks to allow installs from your browser. Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back and tap Install.
  4. If Google Play Protect asks, choose Install anyway. You can skip sending the app for scanning.
  5. Open Nudge and allow notifications, so reminders can reach you.

Samsung phones

Samsung blocks installs from outside the store with Auto Blocker. If the install is blocked:

  1. Open Settings, then Security and privacy, then Auto Blocker, and turn it off.
  2. Install Nudge as described above.
  3. Turn Auto Blocker back on. Nudge keeps working.

Why the warnings?

Android warns about every app that does not come from an app store. Nudge has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. See the privacy policy.

Use it on your computer too

If you turn on sync in the app, your notes are also available at the Nudge web page.
