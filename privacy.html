Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Nudge is a personal notes and reminders app (Android app and this web page). It is built for a single user and is not offered commercially.

What Nudge stores

Your notes, reminder times and their status. On the phone they are stored locally on the device. If you turn on sync, an encrypted copy is stored in a hidden, app-only folder (appDataFolder) in your own Google Drive.

Encryption

Before anything leaves your device, notes are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from a passphrase only you know (PBKDF2-SHA256, 600,000 iterations). The passphrase and key are never sent anywhere. Google, the developer and anyone else see only unreadable data.

Google account access

Nudge requests one Google permission: drive.appdata, which allows access only to Nudge's own hidden folder in your Drive. It cannot see your other files, email or contacts. Nudge's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What Nudge does not do

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party servers. Nudge talks only to Google, and only to read and write your encrypted files. Nothing is sold or shared.

Deleting your data

Turn off sync in the app, or remove Nudge's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions; you can delete Nudge's hidden data from Google Drive settings under "Manage apps". Uninstalling the app deletes the notes on the phone.
